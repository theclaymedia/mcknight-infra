About us

One of Cumbria's most prominent luxury home builders

McKnight Homes is an independent Carlisle based company and one of Cumbria's most prominent luxury home developers. Over the years we have developed a reputation for an uncompromising approach to building exceptional homes that homeowners can trust.

Our developments are built in prime locations across the region, with each property uniquely designed to complement the outstanding aspects of the area. Whether we're breathing new life into old buildings or transforming pockets of Cumbria into remarkable countryside developments, our work is characterised by originality and expert craftsmanship.

Our experience, expertise and enthusiasm creates beautiful homes.
