Service-disabled veteran leadership

The discipline that got us home

McKnight is owned and operated by service-disabled veterans. Our military training in mission-critical operations and disciplined project management delivers dependable results on every job, the same approach that saw us through deployment overseas.

  • Set-aside contract ready

    Verified by the U.S. government as an SDVOSB, we qualify for federal and state set-aside contracts, often giving priority on government and large institutional projects.

  • Clearance ready teams

    Recent security clearances and proven protocols for sensitive and secure sites.

  • Zero delays, zero turnover

    A perfect record of finishing mission-critical jobs with no delays or crew turnover.

Bottom line: you get the reliability of a military-trained team, plus real advantages when bidding on larger commercial or government-backed projects.

Construction and technical infrastructure, one contractor
